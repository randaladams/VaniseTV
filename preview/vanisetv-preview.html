About v1.0.0

Channel Editor

OK shows or hides a channel. Hidden channels disappear from Live TV and from the top 3 tiles. Changes are saved to the local channel file right away.

Refresh channels?

This downloads both channel sources, combines them without duplicates and replaces the local copy. Channels you hid stay hidden if they are still in the new list.
